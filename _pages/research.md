My research is centered on **experimental fluid mechanics**. This page intentionally stays at the level of broad, public research themes.

## Research themes

- **Turbulence and hydrodynamic instabilities**
- **Fluid-structure interactions** and freely moving anisotropic bodies
- **Multiphase and particle-laden flows**
- **Collective dynamics and multiscale organization** in dispersed systems

## Approach

Experimental measurements, quantitative image analysis, Lagrangian tracking, and statistical data analysis.

For concrete results and methods, see my [published work]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
